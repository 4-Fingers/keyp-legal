Delete your Keyp account

App: Keyp · Developer: Corrie Vermaak (South Africa) · Last updated: 6 October 2026

Keyp works without any account. A cloud account exists only if you chose the optional cloud backup or the keeper alarm. This page explains how to delete that cloud account and everything attached to it.

Option 1: Delete it inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Keyp and unlock it.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Cloud sync.
  4. Tap Delete my cloud account.
  5. Confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

The deletion happens straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Use this if you cannot open the app, or no longer have the phone.

  1. Email customedge.sa@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Keyp cloud account.
  2. Send it from the email address you used for the cloud account. This is how we know the request is yours.
  3. We will delete the account and reply to confirm, within 7 days and in any case within 30 days.

Do not put your master password in the email. We never need it, and we cannot use it.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud account (the email address and sign-in).
  • All your encrypted backup in the cloud: records, document files, and the key material that goes with them.
  • Your keeper alarm, if you had one: the display name, check-in times, countdown settings, and the encrypted half of the Legacy Key. After this, your partner's keeper code no longer works.

What is NOT deleted

  • Data stored only on your phone. Your vault stays on the phone. We cannot reach it. To remove it, uninstall Keyp, or clear its data in Android (Settings → Apps → Keyp → Storage → Clear data). Either one erases the vault from the phone.
  • Copies you made yourself, such as an exported .keypbackup file, a shared document, or a saved copy. Delete those yourself.
  • Your Google Play purchases and subscription. These are handled by Google. Cancel a subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting a Keyp cloud account does not cancel it.
  • Your partner's own Keyp app. A keeper's app is theirs. It keeps its own keeper code until they remove it. If you delete your account, that code simply stops working.

How long things stay after deletion

Deletion removes the data from our live database straight away. The database provider (Supabase) may keep routine system backups or logs for a short period under its own schedule. They are not used for anything else and are removed when that schedule runs out. We keep no copy of our own. If you email us, we may keep that email and our reply for a short time to prove the request was handled.

Before you delete

  • Deleting a cloud account cannot be undone.
  • If you want to keep your vault, make sure it is safe on your phone, and export an encrypted backup first (Settings → Export encrypted backup).
  • If you use the keeper alarm, tell your partner it will stop working.
